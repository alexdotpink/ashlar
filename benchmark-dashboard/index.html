Measurement context

What the numbers mean

The dashboard is a concise view of the raw benchmark contracts, not a replacement for the evidence.

01

Same-machine pairing

Each pull request measures main and the candidate sequentially on the Pink VPS. Compatible environments are required before comparison.

02

Percentiles, not vibes

p50 is the typical operation. p95 exposes the slower tail. Allocation measures bytes created per operation. Throughput measures operations per second.

03

Confidence matters

A point estimate can move while its confidence range still crosses zero. That is evidence of noise, not proof of a regression. Contract status remains visible everywhere.
