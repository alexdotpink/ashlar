# Author benchmarks as framework scenarios

Plug-in authors will declare benchmarks through one small Kotlin `setup`/`measure`/`verify` scenario DSL with typed command, event, input, item, and menu fixtures rather than separate top-level languages for each module. Every journey requires semantic verification outside its timed boundary, and isolated ashlar-overhead cases require equivalence with their matched control. Layer-specific runners may use specialized JVM or live-server engines, but engine lifecycle, warmup, sampling, environment capture, and comparison remain outside ordinary scenario code; annotated benchmark methods and raw engine APIs remain internal escape hatches rather than the primary authoring model.
